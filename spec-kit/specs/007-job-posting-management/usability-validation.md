# Recruiter Header Usability Validation

The required study protocol is intentionally not fabricated in source control.

- Cohort: exactly 20 eligible, uncoached participants.
- Device split: 10 primarily mobile and 10 primarily desktop/laptop.
- Assignment: exactly five participants per confirmed header state.
- Measure: five-second state identification, raw elapsed time, both answers, and pass/fail only.
- Release threshold: at least 18 of 20 correct.
