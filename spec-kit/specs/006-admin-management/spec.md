## User Scenarios & Testing _(mandatory)_

### User Story 1 - Enter the Protected Administration Console (Priority: P1)

As a Platform Administrator, I want a separate administration entry point with strong access checks so that ordinary candidates and recruiters cannot see or invoke platform-management capabilities.

**Why this priority**: Every other administration capability depends on a trustworthy platform-level authorization boundary. A visually hidden link is not sufficient protection.

**Independent Test**: Visit the administration origin while signed out, as an ordinary Candidate, as a recruiter-enabled user, as a suspended administrator, and as an active Platform Administrator with and without the required second factor; verify that only the fully authorized administrator reaches the console and that denied attempts reveal no administrative data.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an active account with an active Platform Administrator grant and completed two-factor authentication, **When** the user signs in at `console.admin.localhost:3001`, **Then** the administration console opens and displays only capabilities authorized for Platform Administrators.
2. **Given** an ordinary Candidate or a user with one or more active company memberships but no Platform Administrator grant, **When** the user visits the administration origin or calls an administration operation directly, **Then** access is denied before administrative content or counts are returned.
3. **Given** an administrator whose account or administrator grant is suspended, expired, or revoked, **When** an existing session requests any administration page or action, **Then** access is denied immediately according to the changed state.
4. **Given** an administrator who has not completed the required second-factor step, **When** the user attempts to enter the console, **Then** the user is directed through the approved secure authentication flow without creating another browser-session mechanism.
5. **Given** an authenticated Platform Administrator, **When** the user visits a Candidate or Recruiter workspace, **Then** the account's base Candidate identity and any valid company memberships continue to follow their own permissions and do not inherit administrator access.
6. **Given** an administrator already has one administration-authorized session, **When** the same administrator completes administration sign-in and two-factor authentication in another browser or device, **Then** the new session becomes the sole administration-authorized session and the previous administration-authorized session is revoked within 2 seconds; unrelated Candidate-only sessions for that account remain subject to the ordinary account session policy and cannot enter the administration console without completing administration sign-in.
7. **Given** an administration-authorized session whose most recent successful two-factor proof is more than 15 minutes old, **When** the administrator attempts to view or download business-license evidence, revoke one or all user sessions, suspend or reinstate an account, approve/request changes/reject an employer-verification request, suspend/restore/remove a company membership, or confirm report-linked enforcement, **Then** the action is paused until a new two-factor proof succeeds; failed or abandoned proof leaves business state unchanged, while read-only console navigation remains available.

### User Story 2 - Understand Platform and Review Workload (Priority: P1)

As a Platform Administrator, I want a clear dashboard of account, recruiter-membership, verification, and moderation totals so that I can understand the platform and prioritize urgent work.

**Why this priority**: Administrators need a reliable starting point and must not confuse people, memberships, and roles when one person can belong to multiple companies.

**Independent Test**: Seed accounts with overlapping Candidate identities, multiple company memberships, different account and membership states, pending verification requests, and moderation reports; verify every card and drill-down against the seeded truth without double-counting people as memberships.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** every normal account has a base Candidate identity, including recruiter-enabled users, **When** the dashboard loads, **Then** it labels Candidate accounts and unique recruiter-enabled accounts as overlapping populations rather than presenting them as mutually exclusive roles.
2. **Given** one person has active memberships in two companies, **When** totals are displayed, **Then** the person counts once in the unique recruiter-enabled-user total and twice in the active-membership total.
3. **Given** memberships with OWNER, HR_MANAGER, RECRUITER, and HIRING_MANAGER authority, **When** the role breakdown is displayed, **Then** it counts active memberships by company role and clearly labels the unit as memberships, not people.
4. **Given** pending business-verification requests and pending moderation reports, **When** the dashboard loads, **Then** it shows actionable queue totals and provides filtered paths into each queue.
5. **Given** suspended accounts, suspended memberships, and removed memberships, **When** state summaries are shown, **Then** each lifecycle type is reported separately and can be filtered without exposing private documents or report details in aggregate cards.
6. **Given** a dashboard aggregate was calculated and its source data changes before the administrator opens a drill-down, **When** the drill-down loads, **Then** the aggregate continues to show its calculation timestamp and a notice that it may be up to 60 seconds old, while the drill-down shows its own calculation timestamp and current matching results; differing totals are not presented as an error or silently forced to match.

### User Story 3 - Manage Accounts and Sessions Safely (Priority: P1)

As a Platform Administrator, I want to find a user, inspect safe account and session information, suspend or reinstate the account, and revoke selected sessions so that I can respond to compromise, abuse, or support incidents.

**Why this priority**: Account suspension and session revocation are immediate safety controls, but mistakes can lock out legitimate users or expose security data.

**Independent Test**: Search for several accounts, revoke one session, revoke all sessions, suspend an active account, attempt authentication from old and new sessions, reinstate the account, and verify audit, notification, privacy, concurrency, and self-protection behavior.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** many accounts, **When** an administrator searches by an exact account reference, name, or normalized email and filters by account or recruiter-membership state, **Then** each result displays exactly the account-search fields listed in FR-014 and follows the page size and ordering in FR-013.
2. **Given** a selected account with active sessions, **When** the administrator opens its security view, **Then** device description, approximate location when available, creation time, last activity, and expiry are shown without session tokens, complete IP addresses, authentication secrets, or raw browser headers.
3. **Given** a selected active session and a reason category plus a 10–500-character administrator explanation, **When** the administrator confirms revocation, **Then** that session loses authenticated access while the account's other sessions remain usable.
4. **Given** a credible compromise and a reason category plus a 10–500-character administrator explanation, **When** the administrator confirms revocation of all sessions, **Then** every existing session for the target account becomes unusable and the user must authenticate again.
5. **Given** an ACTIVE account and a reason category plus a 10–500-character administrator explanation, **When** the administrator confirms suspension, **Then** the account becomes SUSPENDED, all sessions and unfinished authentication challenges become unusable, new login is blocked, and unrelated account data is preserved.
6. **Given** a SUSPENDED account and a reason category plus a 10–500-character administrator explanation, **When** the administrator confirms reinstatement, **Then** the account becomes ACTIVE but prior sessions remain revoked and independently suspended or removed company memberships are not restored.
7. **Given** two administrators act on the same account state concurrently, **When** their actions conflict, **Then** at most one transition is accepted for the state version they reviewed and the other administrator receives the current state before retrying.
8. **Given** an administrator selects their own account or the last usable Platform Administrator, **When** an action could remove the current administration access, **Then** the action is blocked and audited to prevent accidental platform lockout.
9. **Given** an account suspension, reinstatement, all-session revocation, or administrator-driven membership change has committed and its security notification repeatedly fails, **When** the fifth delivery attempt fails or the notification reaches 24 hours without delivery, **Then** the original access decision remains effective and the account detail plus audit view show `MANUAL_INTERVENTION_REQUIRED`, the last attempt time, and a non-sensitive failure category.
10. **Given** an administrator supplied the required free-text explanation for a session, account, or membership action, **When** the action commits, **Then** the normalized explanation is available only to a currently authorized Platform Administrator with fresh step-up proof through the correlated privileged action detail, is absent from the affected-user notification, audit event, URLs, analytics, and ordinary logs, becomes inaccessible at 365 calendar days, and is deleted within the following 24 hours.

### User Story 4 - Review Recruiter and Company Verification (Priority: P1)

As a Platform Administrator, I want to review a Candidate's company and business-license evidence and approve, request changes, or reject the request so that only legitimate employers receive company-scoped recruitment authority.

**Why this priority**: Employer verification is a P0 trust boundary. Approval controls access to company data, job posting, and candidate information.

**Independent Test**: Submit clean and unsafe documents for new and existing companies, review the resulting requests, exercise approve/change-request/reject decisions, simulate duplicate and concurrent requests, and verify company membership, notification, document access, and audit outcomes.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an authenticated Candidate with no active or pending request for the same company, **When** the user submits a valid company name, a Vietnamese tax identifier that satisfies the exact FR-025 ten-digit rule, and a supported business-license document, **Then** the user receives a private PENDING_CHECKS request and the administrator queue receives it only after all four FR-026 checks pass.
2. **Given** a document that is unsupported, over 5,000,000 bytes, infected, structurally invalid, or indeterminate after the FR-026 deadline, **When** it is submitted or processed, **Then** employer privileges are not granted, its content is not made available to an administrator, and the user sees the resulting request state plus the permitted replace-or-resubmit action.
3. **Given** a PENDING_REVIEW request and an administrator with fresh step-up proof, **When** the administrator opens it, **Then** the administrator can see the submitted company facts, existing company or membership matches, evidence that passed FR-026, submission history, and decision history without receiving a public document URL.
4. **Given** evidence that is incomplete but potentially correctable, **When** the administrator requests changes using 10–500 characters of applicant-visible guidance, **Then** the request remains non-approved, the applicant is notified, and up to three resubmissions can be associated with the same review history.
5. **Given** evidence that is invalid or violates policy, **When** the administrator rejects the request using one FR-031 rejection category, 10–500 characters of applicant-visible reason, and an optional private note of at most 2,000 characters, **Then** no active membership is granted and the applicant receives the state and permitted reapplication action without confidential detection rules or the private note.
6. **Given** a valid request for a company not yet known by its legal tax identifier, **When** the administrator approves it, **Then** the company becomes verified and active and the applicant receives an active OWNER membership for that company.
7. **Given** a valid request for an already verified company, **When** the administrator approves it, **Then** the applicant receives the selected approved company role without replacing the account's base Candidate identity.
8. **Given** the applicant is already an active member of that company or another administrator has just decided the request, **When** approval is attempted, **Then** duplicate authority is not created and the reviewer sees the authoritative outcome.
9. **Given** a successful, rejected, or changes-requested decision, **When** the decision completes, **Then** the request state, company and membership effects, notification work, and audit record agree; a notification-delivery delay does not reverse a committed decision.
10. **Given** a verification request remains in PENDING_CHECKS because safety checking is unavailable, **When** 15 minutes elapse, **Then** the request is marked delayed and the applicant receives one delay notice; if checks still have not completed at 24 hours, the request becomes EXPIRED, no evidence becomes reviewable, the applicant is notified, and a new submission is permitted.
11. **Given** a request is in PENDING_REVIEW but its already-cleared evidence cannot be opened, **When** the protected viewer outage reaches 15 minutes, **Then** review decisions are disabled and the request is operationally escalated; at 24 hours the applicant receives one delay notice, and at 72 continuous hours the request becomes EXPIRED without granting authority and the applicant may submit again.
12. **Given** a verification request targets an already verified company, **When** the applicant has neither a valid unexpired invitation scoped to that applicant, company, and requested role nor explicit approval from a current active OWNER of that company, **Then** the administrator cannot approve the request, no company data or membership is disclosed or granted, and the applicant is directed to obtain one of those prerequisites outside Feature 006.
13. **Given** the applicant's request is in PENDING_CHECKS, PENDING_REVIEW, or CHANGES_REQUESTED and no terminal decision has committed, **When** that applicant cancels it, **Then** exactly one CANCELLED terminal outcome succeeds, no authority is granted, evidence follows FR-027, and an administrator cannot cancel on the applicant's behalf.
14. **Given** a request entered CHANGES_REQUESTED and no accepted resubmission or cancellation occurs, **When** 30 calendar days have elapsed from the changes-requested time, **Then** it becomes EXPIRED, no authority is granted, evidence follows FR-027, the applicant receives the FR-037 expiry notification, and a new request is permitted.

### User Story 5 - Suspend, Restore, or Remove Recruiter Authority (Priority: P1)

As a Platform Administrator, I want to act on one company membership without disabling the person's Candidate account or unrelated company memberships so that enforcement is proportional and tenant boundaries remain intact.

**Why this priority**: Recruiter authority is company-scoped. Treating it as a global user role would break the project's multi-company model and could remove legitimate access elsewhere.

**Independent Test**: Give one user memberships in two companies, suspend and restore one membership, remove it, and verify Candidate access, the other company, recruiter-console authorization, in-flight actions, audit, and notifications.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a user with an active membership in Company A and a reason category plus a 10–500-character administrator explanation, **When** an administrator suspends that membership, **Then** recruiter access to Company A ends immediately while Candidate access and memberships in other companies remain unchanged.
2. **Given** a suspended Company A membership and a reason category plus a 10–500-character administrator explanation, **When** an administrator restores it, **Then** its prior approved company role becomes active again and access to other identities or memberships is unchanged.
3. **Given** an active or suspended membership and a reason category plus a 10–500-character administrator explanation, **When** an administrator removes it after explicit confirmation, **Then** the user loses that company authority and must undergo a new approved invitation or verification process to regain it.
4. **Given** a user whose last active company membership is suspended or removed, **When** the user next accesses the recruiter origin, **Then** access is denied while the user's base Candidate workspace remains available if the account itself is ACTIVE.
5. **Given** an OWNER membership, **When** an administrator attempts to suspend or remove it while it is the company's last active OWNER, **Then** the action is blocked until ownership is transferred or the company follows its separately authorized lifecycle.
6. **Given** a membership changes while the user has a recruiter operation open, **When** that operation is submitted, **Then** current company membership and role are checked again and the stale operation cannot change company data.

### User Story 6 - Review Candidate, Recruiter, Company, and Job Reports (Priority: P2)

As a Platform Administrator, I want one private moderation queue for reports about jobs, recruiter memberships or companies, and Candidates connected to a legitimate recruitment interaction so that I can investigate violations without giving report submitters enforcement power.

**Why this priority**: The system already accepts private job reports, and administrators need a usable review destination. Actor reports add value only when their relationship and privacy boundaries are explicit.

**Independent Test**: Submit reports from candidate-only and recruiter-enabled accounts, verify relationship rules for Candidate and recruiter reports, review/filter/assign the queue, resolve or dismiss reports, and prove that report submission alone never suspends an account, membership, company, or job.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an existing job report, **When** the administration queue opens, **Then** it appears with target reference, category, status, submission time, reporter account reference, current reporter account state, and qualifying relationship reference without changing the job's publication state.
2. **Given** a Candidate viewing a public job, **When** the Candidate submits one FR-048 category and compliant detail about the related job, company, or recruiter, **Then** one private moderation report is created unless the dedupe or quota rule applies, and the reporter receives the response required by FR-048.
3. **Given** an active company member who is currently authorized to process a specific application, **When** the member reports that Candidate for an allowed safety or conduct reason from that application context, **Then** the report is linked to that application and does not expose the Candidate to unrelated companies.
4. **Given** a user without a qualifying public-job, company, or application relationship, **When** the user attempts to report an arbitrary Candidate or private recruiter membership, **Then** the attempt is denied without revealing whether the target exists.
5. **Given** pending reports, **When** an administrator filters by report target, reason, priority, status, company, or age, **Then** results follow the page size and deterministic order in FR-050 and are visible only to currently authorized Platform Administrators.
6. **Given** a PENDING_REVIEW report, **When** an administrator assigns it, investigates it, or records an optional plain-text private note of at most 2,000 Unicode characters after normalization, **Then** it remains PENDING_REVIEW; **When** an administrator dismisses or resolves it, **Then** it enters the selected terminal state, cannot be reopened, preserves its history and any separately confirmed enforcement link, and prevents concurrent reviewers from silently overwriting the outcome.
7. **Given** any submitted report, **When** no administrator has made an enforcement decision, **Then** the report alone does not remove a job, suspend an account, suspend a membership, or disadvantage a Candidate in recruitment scoring or pipeline decisions.
8. **Given** a different member of the same company can identify an application but is not currently authorized to process it, **When** that member attempts to report its Candidate, **Then** submission is denied with the same unavailable response used for an unknown application and the response reveals neither the Candidate nor application existence.
9. **Given** a reporter already has an unresolved report or submitted the same target-and-reason combination within the preceding 24 hours, **When** the reporter repeats it, **Then** no new report is created and the reporter receives the same neutral acknowledgement as the initial accepted submission; after 10 accepted new reports across all targets in a rolling 24-hour window, additional new reports are denied with a retry-after duration and no target-existence disclosure.

3. **Given** a user with multiple active memberships, **When** one company context is selected, **Then** every recruiter operation remains limited to that company and role.

### User Story 8 - Resolve Support Requests Through Direct Messaging (Priority: P1)

As an authenticated active account holder, I want to open a private support case with SmartHire Support and receive replies in the product so that routine account, profile, recruitment, messaging, privacy, and safety questions do not require an email exchange.

As a Platform Administrator, I want a protected Support Inbox where I can claim, reassign, reply to, annotate, resolve, and close support cases so that the platform can provide accountable assistance without granting access to ordinary user-to-user conversations.

**Why this priority**: Direct support reduces resolution time while preserving the existing rule that Platform Administrators are not unrestricted readers of Feature 008 conversations.

**Independent Test**: Create cases as active users, exceed case and message limits, claim and race-claim cases as two administrators, reassign between administrators, add internal notes, exchange realtime messages, resolve, reopen inside seven days, auto-close after seven days, revoke an assignee's administrator authority, and execute retention cleanup; verify authorization, neutral errors, audit, notification, privacy, and concurrency outcomes.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an authenticated ACTIVE account with fewer than three non-terminal cases and fewer than five new cases in the preceding 24 hours, **When** the user submits an allowlisted category, a 5–120-character subject, and a 1–4,000-character initial plain-text message, **Then** one private support case is created in WAITING_FOR_SUPPORT, appears in the Support Inbox, and is represented to the user only as a conversation with `SmartHire Support`.
2. **Given** an unauthorized, suspended, deleted, cross-origin, forged, or over-limit requester, **When** support data or a mutation is requested, **Then** no support content or target existence is disclosed and no case or message is created.
3. **Given** an unassigned support case, **When** two authorized administrators claim the same reviewed version concurrently, **Then** exactly one becomes the current assignee and the other receives the authoritative current version before retrying.
4. **Given** an assigned case, **When** an authorized administrator reassigns it with a required allowlisted reason, **Then** exactly one active assignment remains, the prior assignment receives an immutable end record, the user-facing state does not change, and the user continues to see only `SmartHire Support`.
5. **Given** an assigned non-closed case, **When** its current assignee sends a valid reply, **Then** the reply is durably persisted before acknowledgement, the state becomes WAITING_FOR_USER, the user receives a realtime invalidation plus a content-free email notification, and other administrators cannot impersonate the assignee through direct operations.
6. **Given** a requester sends a valid message to a non-closed case, **When** the write commits, **Then** the state becomes WAITING_FOR_SUPPORT, the Support Inbox receives a realtime invalidation, and no ordinary Feature 008 conversation, eligibility record, presence state, or message is created.
7. **Given** an administrator adds a private note, **When** any requester API, realtime event, notification, browser cache, URL, analytics event, or ordinary log is inspected, **Then** the note and its existence are absent; it remains available only through the protected admin case detail.
8. **Given** an assigned case is resolved, **When** the requester replies within seven calendar days, **Then** the same case reopens as WAITING_FOR_SUPPORT; **When** no reply occurs by the deadline, **Then** the worker transitions it once to terminal CLOSED.
9. **Given** a case is CLOSED, **When** either party attempts to send another message or reopen it, **Then** the operation is rejected and the requester is directed to create a new case.
10. **Given** the current assignee loses ACTIVE account or Platform Administrator authority, **When** the worker or next case operation observes the change, **Then** the assignment is ended as AUTHORITY_LOST, the case returns to the unassigned queue without exposing administrator identity, and the support state remains accurate.
11. **Given** a case has been CLOSED for 365 calendar days, **When** retention processing runs, **Then** user-visible messages and internal notes become inaccessible and are deleted within 24 hours while content-free lifecycle and audit metadata remain.
12. **Given** any Platform Administrator uses Support Inbox, **When** they attempt to inspect an ordinary Feature 008 conversation through support or admin APIs, **Then** no endpoint, resource, query, or UI path returns that conversation or its messages.

### Edge Cases

- A Platform Administrator grant changes while the administrator has the console open; every subsequent page and action rechecks current authority.
- A target account becomes suspended between the administrator's review and session-revocation action; the result reflects the current state and does not recreate access.
- The last usable administrator cannot self-suspend, revoke their own administrator grant, or make the platform unmanageable through this console.
- An account has no active sessions; revoke-one is unavailable and revoke-all completes idempotently without claiming that sessions existed.
- Reinstating an account does not restore deleted sessions, removed memberships, rejected verification requests, removed jobs, or other independently changed state.
- A user belongs to several companies with different roles and states; every display distinguishes user counts from membership counts and every action names the target company.
- Two verification applicants submit the same normalized tax identifier concurrently; at most one legal-company identity is established and both requests remain traceable.
- A license is replaced after changes were requested; administrators review the current version while prior versions remain protected according to the approved evidence policy.
- A verification decision occurs as its evidence expires or is deleted; approval fails safely unless the required evidence was available and valid at the decision boundary.
- Safety checking that remains unavailable for 15 minutes marks a request delayed and reaches EXPIRED at 24 hours; protected-viewer unavailability blocks decisions, escalates at 15 minutes, notifies the applicant at 24 hours, and reaches EXPIRED at 72 continuous hours; neither path grants authority.
- A report target is later suspended, removed, deleted, or made inaccessible; the report history retains only the references and timestamps listed in FR-052 without restoring or exposing the target.
- A report contains markup, executable or style content, ASCII control characters, Unicode bidirectional controls, personal data, or attempts to influence administrators; required normalization and display rules apply, content above 2,000 characters is rejected, and report text is excluded from ordinary logs.
- Removing or suspending an OWNER is blocked when it would leave an active company without an active OWNER unless a separately authorized company action resolves ownership.
- Hostname manipulation, direct operation calls, stale browser state, or a recruiter-domain page cannot substitute for server-verified Platform Administrator or company-membership authority.
- A support request is retried with the same requester and idempotency key; exactly one case or message is visible and an incompatible retry is rejected.
- A resolved support case receives a requester message at the exact auto-close boundary; row-version and deadline checks permit exactly one authoritative reopen-or-close outcome.
- An administrator is reassigned while composing a reply; the send revalidates current assignment and stale versions before persisting content.
- Realtime delivery fails after a support write commits; HTTP remains authoritative and reconnect triggers a protected refetch without duplicate messages.
- Support content contains markup, executable/style content, control characters, bidirectional controls, or excessive line breaks; FR-048 normalization applies and over-limit text is rejected without truncation.

## Requirements _(mandatory)_

### Functional Requirements

#### Administration Access and Separation

- **FR-001**: The system MUST represent Platform Administrator authority separately from the base Candidate identity and from all company memberships.
- **FR-002**: Platform Administrator authority MUST NOT be obtainable through public registration, employer verification, company ownership, company role changes, or a client-supplied role value.
- **FR-003**: The local administration entry point MUST be `console.admin.localhost:3001`. Each non-local environment MUST define exactly one administration origin before startup; a request using any other origin or host MUST be denied before administration content is returned.
- **FR-004**: Every administration page, read, and mutation MUST require a current ACTIVE account, a current active Platform Administrator grant, the sole administration-authorized session defined by FR-062, and successful two-factor authentication for that session. The initial two-factor proof authorizes console entry and starts a 15-minute step-up window. Viewing or downloading business-license evidence; revoking one or all user sessions; suspending or reinstating an account; approving, requesting changes for, or rejecting employer verification; suspending, restoring, or removing a company membership; and confirming report-linked enforcement MUST require a successful two-factor proof within the preceding 15 minutes. Expiry of that window MUST leave read-only console access available but MUST block those actions until proof is renewed.
- **FR-005**: All authorization MUST be enforced before protected data is returned and MUST NOT trust the requested hostname, hidden navigation, client state, dashboard labels, or prior authorization results as proof of authority.
- **FR-006**: Unauthorized, expired, revoked, suspended, cross-origin, and forged administration requests MUST return no administration payload, make no business-state change, and reveal none of the following: administrative counts, target existence, business documents, reports, or audit details.
- **FR-007**: Administration pages MUST use a distinct, clearly labelled console shell and MUST remain keyboard operable, screen-reader understandable, visibly focused, and usable for data-dense desktop work without depending on color alone.
- **FR-008**: Administrator access changes and denied privileged attempts MUST take effect on the next request and MUST be auditable.
- **FR-009**: This feature MUST prevent an administrator from suspending their own account or removing the last usable Platform Administrator through the console.

#### Dashboard and Search

- **FR-010**: The dashboard MUST show total Candidate identities by account state, unique accounts with at least one active company membership, active company memberships by role, suspended memberships, pending recruiter-verification requests, and pending moderation reports from a periodic snapshot calculated no more than 60 seconds before display.
- **FR-011**: Every dashboard metric MUST define and display whether it counts people, accounts, companies, memberships, requests, or reports; recruiter-enabled accounts MUST be identified as a subset that still retains Candidate identity.
- **FR-012**: Dashboard cards MUST always display their snapshot calculation time and the statement "Counts may be up to 60 seconds old." A drill-down MUST calculate its own current result when opened, display its own calculation time, and show "Source data changed after the dashboard snapshot" whenever its total differs from the originating card. Cards and drill-downs MUST use identical state definitions but MUST NOT conceal, overwrite, or treat a timestamp-based difference as an error.
- **FR-013**: Administrators MUST be able to search accounts by exact account reference, name, or normalized email and filter by account state, recruiter-enabled state, membership role, and membership state. Results MUST default to 25 items per page, permit at most 100 items per page, and use account creation time descending followed by account reference ascending as the deterministic tie-break order.
- **FR-014**: Each account-search row MUST contain only account reference, display name, masked email, account state, creation date, active-membership count, and whether an active Platform Administrator grant exists. Email masking MUST preserve the domain, preserve only the first Unicode character of a local part containing two or more characters, replace the rest of that local part with `***`, and display `***` for a one-character local part. The row MUST NOT include credential data, document contents, complete IP addresses, private Candidate profile content, or unrelated company data.

#### Account and Session Management

- **FR-015**: An account detail MUST show account reference, display name, masked email, account state and last state-change time, creation time, each active membership's company reference/role/state, and each Login Session field allowed by the Key Entities section. No other credential, Candidate-profile, document, or session field may appear in this view.
- **FR-016**: Administrators MUST be able to revoke one target-account session or all target-account sessions after explicit confirmation, selection of exactly one reason category from SECURITY_COMPROMISE, POLICY_VIOLATION, USER_REQUEST, VERIFICATION_FAILURE, INCIDENT_RESOLVED, ACCESS_CLEANUP, or OTHER, and entry of a required administrator explanation containing 10–500 Unicode characters after the FR-048 plain-text normalization rules. The explanation MUST follow the privileged visibility and 365-day retention policy in FR-021.
- **FR-017**: Session management MUST never expose session credentials, reusable identifiers, full network addresses, factor secrets, submitted codes, or raw browser headers.
- **FR-018**: Administrators MUST be able to transition an ACTIVE account to SUSPENDED only after explicit confirmation, selection of exactly one FR-016 reason category, and entry of a required 10–500-character administrator explanation governed by the FR-016 normalization and FR-021 privacy/retention rules; suspension MUST invalidate all sessions and unfinished authentication challenges and block every new authenticated workflow.
- **FR-019**: Administrators MUST be able to transition a SUSPENDED account to ACTIVE only after explicit confirmation, selection of exactly one FR-016 reason category, and entry of a required 10–500-character administrator explanation governed by the FR-016 normalization and FR-021 privacy/retention rules; reinstatement MUST NOT recreate sessions or restore independently suspended or removed memberships and content.
- **FR-020**: Account suspension, reinstatement, and session revocation MUST be idempotent, concurrency-safe, and based on current authoritative state; stale conflicting actions MUST not report success.
- **FR-021**: Account lifecycle, session, and administrator-driven membership actions MUST record actor, target, action, prior and resulting state where applicable, the selected FR-016 reason category, result, timestamp, and correlation reference without storing the free-text administrator explanation, secrets, or unnecessary personal data in the audit event. The normalized explanation MUST instead be retained as a separate Privileged Action Rationale linked by the correlation reference, visible only to a currently authorized Platform Administrator with a two-factor proof no more than 15 minutes old. It MUST NOT appear in the affected-user notification, URLs, analytics, or ordinary logs; it MUST become inaccessible exactly 365 calendar days after the action timestamp and be deleted within the following 24 hours.
- **FR-022**: Account suspension, reinstatement, all-session revocation, and administrator-driven membership suspension, restoration, or removal MUST create one security notification when the action commits. The first delivery attempt MUST be due immediately; attempt 2 MUST be due 1 minute after attempt 1 fails, attempt 3 5 minutes after attempt 2 fails, attempt 4 30 minutes after attempt 3 fails, and attempt 5 2 hours after attempt 4 fails. A rejected or nonexistent destination, disabled destination, invalid generated notification content, or delivery-policy refusal that cannot succeed unchanged MUST be classified as permanent and skip further automatic attempts; all other delivery failures MUST use the retry schedule. Failure of attempt 5, any permanent failure, or reaching 24 hours without delivery MUST set `MANUAL_INTERVENTION_REQUIRED`. The original access decision MUST remain effective. The administrator's account detail and audit view MUST show PENDING, RETRYING, DELIVERED, or MANUAL_INTERVENTION_REQUIRED, the last attempt time, next attempt time when applicable, and one non-sensitive failure category from DESTINATION_REJECTED, DESTINATION_DISABLED, CONTENT_INVALID, POLICY_REFUSED, TEMPORARY_UNAVAILABLE, or ATTEMPTS_EXHAUSTED.

#### Recruiter Verification

- **FR-024**: An authenticated Candidate MUST be able to submit one active employer-verification request per normalized company tax identifier using company facts and one business-license document. A request targeting an already verified company MUST identify either an invitation that is unexpired, unrevoked, unused, and scoped to the exact applicant, company, and requested role or explicit approval recorded for that exact request by a current active OWNER of that company. The selected prerequisite MUST still satisfy every condition at the administrator's decision boundary; a tax-identifier match alone MUST NOT satisfy this relationship prerequisite or disclose company-private data.
- **FR-025**: Employer-verification submission MUST remove leading and trailing Unicode whitespace from the submitted Vietnamese company tax identifier and then require exactly 10 ASCII digits (`0`–`9`). The resulting unchanged ten-digit sequence is the normalized identifier used for matching and uniqueness. Embedded whitespace, separators, letters, non-ASCII digits, fewer or more than 10 digits, and an empty value MUST be rejected before document processing. The submission MUST accept only PDF, PNG, or JPEG documents containing 1–5,000,000 bytes inclusive.
- **FR-026**: A submitted business document MUST be isolated and pass malware, declared-versus-detected file-type, structural-integrity, and preview-safety checks before it becomes reviewable; unsafe or indeterminate content MUST never grant privileges or reach an administrator preview. A request still in PENDING_CHECKS at 15 minutes MUST be marked delayed and create exactly one applicant delay notification. A request still in PENDING_CHECKS at 24 hours MUST transition to EXPIRED, create exactly one expiry notification, make its evidence inaccessible, and permit a new submission.
- **FR-027**: Business documents and derived previews MUST be excluded from public URLs and ordinary logs and available only to the applicant while their request is non-terminal and to an administrator with current review authority and fresh step-up proof. Superseded evidence versions and evidence for REJECTED, CANCELLED, or EXPIRED requests MUST become inaccessible immediately and be deleted within 24 hours. Evidence for APPROVED requests MUST remain available while the associated company verification is active; after it is superseded or no longer active, it MUST become inaccessible immediately and be deleted within 30 days.
- **FR-028**: Verification requests MUST support the following transitions only: PENDING_CHECKS to PENDING_REVIEW, CANCELLED, or EXPIRED; PENDING_REVIEW to CHANGES_REQUESTED, APPROVED, REJECTED, CANCELLED, or EXPIRED; CHANGES_REQUESTED to RESUBMITTED, CANCELLED, or EXPIRED; and RESUBMITTED to PENDING_CHECKS. Only the applicant MAY cause CANCELLED, and only from PENDING_CHECKS, PENDING_REVIEW, or CHANGES_REQUESTED before another terminal outcome commits; an administrator MUST NOT cancel on the applicant's behalf. An accepted replacement submission MUST pass from CHANGES_REQUESTED through RESUBMITTED to PENDING_CHECKS as one uninterrupted business outcome, so RESUBMITTED is never exposed as an actionable state. CHANGES_REQUESTED MUST transition to EXPIRED after 30 calendar days without an accepted resubmission or applicant cancellation. APPROVED, REJECTED, CANCELLED, and EXPIRED are terminal for that request. If already-cleared evidence cannot be opened continuously, review decisions MUST be disabled, operational escalation MUST occur at 15 minutes, one applicant delay notification MUST be created at 24 hours, and the request MUST transition from PENDING_REVIEW to EXPIRED at 72 hours without granting authority.
- **FR-029**: The review queue MUST filter by request state, age, company, normalized tax identifier, applicant, and review assignment without exposing document content in list rows. It MUST default to 25 items per page, permit at most 100 items per page, and order oldest actionable request first followed by request reference ascending.
- **FR-030**: A reviewer MUST be able to inspect current submitted facts, relevant existing-company and membership matches, the validity and scope of the FR-024 relationship prerequisite when the company already exists, only evidence that completed FR-026 checks, prior applicant submissions, and decision history before acting.
- **FR-031**: A reviewer MUST be able to request changes using 10–500 characters of applicant-visible guidance; reject using exactly one category from DOCUMENT_UNREADABLE, TAX_ID_MISMATCH, DOCUMENT_EXPIRED, COMPANY_INFORMATION_MISMATCH, DUPLICATE_OR_CONFLICTING_REQUEST, POLICY_INELIGIBLE, or OTHER plus 10–500 characters of applicant-visible reason; add an optional private note of at most 2,000 Unicode characters after the FR-048 normalization rules; or approve with OWNER, HR_MANAGER, RECRUITER, or HIRING_MANAGER. One request MUST accept at most three resubmissions; after the third resubmission, another request-for-changes action MUST be unavailable and the reviewer must approve or reject.
- **FR-032**: Approval for a new legal company MUST atomically establish the verified active company, create the applicant's active OWNER membership, finalize the request, create required notification work, and record the decision audit.
- **FR-033**: Approval for an existing verified company MUST be unavailable unless the FR-024 relationship prerequisite is valid at the decision boundary. A permitted approval using an invitation MUST mark that invitation used so it cannot authorize another request; a permitted approval using OWNER approval MUST mark that request-specific approval fulfilled. The same business outcome MUST create or restore only the approved membership and role, avoid a duplicate company or membership, and leave the user's base Candidate identity unchanged.
- **FR-034**: The exact FR-025 normalized tax identifier, company establishment, membership grant, and verification decisions MUST remain correct under duplicate submission, retry, and concurrent-review conditions.
- **FR-035**: A user who already has an active membership or active verification request for the same company MUST NOT receive duplicate authority through another request.
- **FR-036**: Review decisions MUST identify the acting administrator reference and preserve submission-version number, prior and resulting state, selected FR-031 category, decision time, and decision result; evidence content, the private note, credentials, and administrator session identifiers MUST NOT be stored in this decision history.
- **FR-037**: Applicant notifications MUST name the resulting state, the decision time, and the next available applicant action for receipt, changes requested, approval, rejection, cancellation, delay, or expiry. They MUST NOT disclose internal fraud signals, private administrator notes, evidence storage locations, or administrator identity.

#### Moderation Reports

- **FR-046**: The administration console MUST provide one private moderation queue containing existing job-post reports and reports about a company or recruiter submitted through a current public-job relationship. A Candidate report MUST originate from a specific application: an active OWNER or HR_MANAGER may report any Candidate application belonging to that company, while an active RECRUITER or HIRING_MANAGER may report only when the current application-processing authorization permits that member to act directly on that application. Membership in the same company without that direct authorization MUST NOT permit Candidate reporting.
- **FR-047**: Before accepting a report about a non-public target, the system MUST revalidate the reporter's ACTIVE account, ACTIVE membership, target-company relationship, and direct application-processing authorization required by FR-046. Failure of any check MUST return the same unavailable outcome as an unknown target and MUST reveal neither Candidate nor application existence.
- **FR-048**: Every report MUST select exactly one category from FRAUD_OR_IMPERSONATION, MISLEADING_CONTENT, DISCRIMINATION_OR_HARASSMENT, ABUSE_OR_THREATS, SPAM_OR_DUPLICATE, PRIVACY_OR_DATA_MISUSE, or OTHER. Optional detail MUST be plain text of at most 2,000 Unicode characters after normalization; OTHER requires 10–2,000 characters. Normalization MUST apply Unicode NFKC, convert CRLF and CR to LF, remove HTML markup plus executable/style blocks and their contents, remove ASCII controls U+0000–U+0008, U+000B–U+000C, U+000E–U+001F and U+007F, remove bidirectional controls U+202A–U+202E and U+2066–U+2069, trim outer whitespace, and collapse three or more consecutive line breaks to two. Empty optional detail becomes absent; over-limit detail is rejected without truncation. At most one unresolved report and at most one newly accepted report in a rolling 24-hour window MAY exist per reporter, target, and category; a duplicate creates no new report and returns the same neutral acknowledgement, "Thanks. Your concern was received for review." A reporter MAY create at most 10 new reports across all targets in a rolling 24-hour window; excess submissions MUST state the remaining retry duration without disclosing target existence. Limits MUST aggregate all sessions of the account.
- **FR-049**: Report submission MUST NOT automatically remove a job, suspend an account or membership, alter a company, change an application stage, or affect Candidate scoring.
- **FR-050**: The queue MUST filter by target type, reason, priority, state, company, age, and assignee. Priority MUST be CRITICAL for ABUSE_OR_THREATS or PRIVACY_OR_DATA_MISUSE, HIGH for FRAUD_OR_IMPERSONATION or DISCRIMINATION_OR_HARASSMENT, and NORMAL for MISLEADING_CONTENT, SPAM_OR_DUPLICATE, or OTHER. It MUST default to 25 items per page, permit at most 100 items per page, and order CRITICAL before HIGH before NORMAL, then oldest submission first, then report reference ascending.
- **FR-051**: Every accepted report MUST begin in PENDING_REVIEW. Assignment, investigation, and adding an optional plain-text private note of at most 2,000 Unicode characters after the FR-048 normalization rules MUST leave it in PENDING_REVIEW. The only report-state transitions are PENDING_REVIEW to RESOLVED or PENDING_REVIEW to DISMISSED; both resulting states are terminal and MUST NOT be reopened in Feature 006. A separately confirmed enforcement action MAY be linked before or after the terminal transition without rewriting the original report or changing its state. An over-limit note MUST be rejected without truncation.
- **FR-052**: Report lifecycle changes MUST be concurrency-safe and preserve actor reference, target type and reference, originating job/application/company reference when applicable, state history, result, and timestamp even if the original target later becomes unavailable; the preserved reference MUST NOT include a display name, email address, report text, or deleted profile content.
- **FR-053**: Report details, reporter identity, private notes, and investigation data MUST be visible only to currently authorized Platform Administrators. The reporter receives only submission status and the neutral receipt; the report target receives no reporter identity, report detail, private note, or investigation data in Feature 006.

### Key Entities

- **Platform Administrator Grant**: Separately provisioned platform authority associated with an account; includes lifecycle state, activation and revocation times, and non-secret provenance. It is neither a Candidate role nor a company membership.
- **User Account**: The existing sign-in identity and lifecycle state. Every normal account retains a Candidate identity; account suspension affects all authenticated contexts.
- **Login Session**: An existing server-controlled authenticated session with display metadata limited to device description, approximate location when available, creation time, last activity time, expiry time, and revocation state. Its credential, complete network address, factor proof, and raw browser headers are never exposed through administration views.
- **Administrator Session Policy**: The one-to-one designation between an active Platform Administrator grant and its sole administration-authorized Login Session. It records designation and revocation times plus the most recent successful two-factor-proof time, permits Candidate-only sessions to coexist under the ordinary account policy, revokes the prior designated session when a new one is designated, and requires a fresh proof after the 15-minute step-up window for the actions listed in FR-004.
- **Company**: A legal employer identity keyed by the exact normalized ten-ASCII-digit Vietnamese tax identifier defined by FR-025, with verification and lifecycle state separate from its public profile fields.
- **Company Membership**: A user's company-scoped OWNER, HR_MANAGER, RECRUITER, or HIRING_MANAGER authority with ACTIVE, SUSPENDED, or REMOVED lifecycle state.
- **Recruiter Verification Request**: A Candidate's application to establish or join a company, including submitted facts, current evidence version, workflow state, assignment, decision, timestamps, delay-notification milestones, continuous viewer-unavailability time, a maximum of three resubmissions, and—when joining an existing company—the type, scope, validity, and consumption state of the FR-024 relationship prerequisite.
- **Business License Evidence**: Private, safety-checked, versioned company-verification evidence governed by purpose-specific access, retention, and deletion rules.
- **Moderation Report**: A private allegation about a job, company/recruiter relationship, or Candidate in an authorized application context, with one of the seven FR-048 categories, normalized detail of at most 2,000 characters, reporter and qualifying relationship context, assignment, notes, history, and a state of PENDING_REVIEW, RESOLVED, or DISMISSED governed by the terminal transition rules in FR-051.
- **Administration Audit Event**: An append-only record of a privileged attempt or outcome with actor, action, target, result, timestamp, correlation reference, and allowlisted non-sensitive context.
- **Privileged Action Rationale**: The FR-048-normalized 10–500-character administrator explanation for a committed session, account, or membership action, linked to its audit event only by correlation reference. It is restricted to current Platform Administrators with fresh step-up proof, excluded from affected-user notifications and ordinary telemetry, inaccessible at 365 calendar days, and deleted within the following 24 hours.
- **Notification Work**: Durable, idempotent delivery intent created with an administration outcome. It records PENDING, RETRYING, DELIVERED, or MANUAL_INTERVENTION_REQUIRED; attempt count; last and next attempt times; a 24-hour delivery deadline; and a non-sensitive failure category. Retryable security notifications receive at most five attempts on the FR-022 schedule, while permanent or exhausted failure is visible to administrators and never reverses the originating decision.
- **Support Conversation**: A private requester-owned support case with category, subject, lifecycle state, administrator-only current assignment, deterministic version/message sequence, activity times, resolution deadline, and retention boundary.
- **Support Message**: A sequenced, durable, idempotent plain-text message authored by the requester or current Platform Administrator assignee. Requester projections replace every administrator sender identity with `SmartHire Support`.
- **Support Assignment**: An append-only assignment interval recording assignee, assigning administrator, start, end, and allowlisted end reason. Exactly one interval may remain active for a case.
- **Support Internal Note**: Administrator-only normalized text associated with a support case and excluded from every requester projection and notification.
- **Support Case History**: Content-free immutable state and command history used for concurrency review, lifecycle audit, and retention evidence.

## Assumptions

- "Ban/unban" means reversible account suspension and reinstatement. Permanent account deletion and legal erasure require a separate retention/deletion feature.
- Every normal account remains a Candidate. "Recruiter" describes one or more active company memberships, so recruiter-enabled users are a subset of Candidate accounts rather than a mutually exclusive global role.
- "Ban/unban recruiter" means suspend or restore one company membership. "Deactivate recruiter back to Candidate" means remove one membership; the user was never stripped of Candidate identity.
- Platform Administrator authority is separately provisioned. Public self-service administrator promotion and administrator-grant management are outside this first administration increment.
- Administrators use one administration-authorized session per grant. Initial two-factor proof permits console entry, the proof remains fresh for sensitive actions for 15 minutes, and no support bypass is introduced.
- Account suspension is indefinite until an authorized reinstatement. Scheduled temporary suspensions and automated bans are deferred.
- Existing job reports are included in the administration moderation queue. Candidate and recruiter/company reports are allowed only through a current public-job relationship or the direct application-processing authorization defined in FR-046–FR-047.
- The current company role set remains OWNER, HR_MANAGER, RECRUITER, and HIRING_MANAGER. Adding custom company roles or permission builders is deferred.
- Feature 006 validates and consumes an existing-company invitation or OWNER approval but does not create invitations, provide OWNER approval screens, or manage company teams.
- Business-license evidence uses the existing malware-checking and private-storage capabilities, but follows the access and deletion deadlines in FR-026–FR-028 rather than Candidate CV or ephemeral job-search retention.
- Dashboard cards are periodic operational snapshots no more than 60 seconds old; drill-downs are independently calculated current views and may legitimately differ when source state changes.
- Local domain names are fixed by the feature description. Production hostnames and routing details are selected during planning without weakening the separate-origin and authorization requirements.
- The Recruiter Manager is the next feature group. Feature 006 establishes only membership entitlement and the recruiter-origin access boundary, not recruiter job, applicant, pipeline, analytics, team, or export screens.

## Dependencies

- Existing exclusive server-controlled authentication, session validation, two-factor authentication, account states, session revocation, audit, notification delivery, and same-origin protections.
- Existing Candidate identity, Company, Company Membership, Job Posting, Job Report, and application relationship data.
- Existing authoritative invitation or OWNER-approval state capable of proving every FR-024 scope, validity, and one-request-use condition; creating invitations, collecting OWNER approval, and company-team management remain outside Feature 006.
- Private file storage, malware scanning, PDF/PNG/JPEG validation for 1–5,000,000-byte documents, and protected document viewing that can expose outage duration to the verification lifecycle.
- A documented policy for business-license evidence retention, applicant access, reviewer access, deletion, and legal handling under applicable Vietnamese personal-data requirements.
- At least one securely provisioned bootstrap Platform Administrator outside public self-service flows.

## Out of Scope

- Public or self-service Platform Administrator registration, administrator invitation, fine-grained administrator-role design, and emergency break-glass procedures.
- Permanent account deletion, personal-data erasure, email reuse, password/factor bypass, or editing a user's Candidate profile.
- Automatic suspension based only on report volume, automated fraud scoring, or AI-made moderation and verification decisions.
- Full company deactivation, company deletion, ownership transfer UI, custom company roles, recruiter invitations, or company-team administration.
- Full Recruiter Manager capabilities at `console.recruiter.localhost:3001`, including job-post management, applicant review, scoring, pipeline/Kanban, notifications, analytics, and export.
- Recruitment analytics and data export beyond operational counts required to run the administration queues.
- Job-post approval and removal workflow beyond viewing current job reports and linking a separately authorized enforcement action; full job-post moderation can be specified as its own focused increment.
- Reading, searching, exporting, moderating, or impersonating any ordinary Feature 008 conversation through Support Center.
- Support attachments, anonymous visitor chat, voice/video, bots, AI replies, cross-case merging, public knowledge base, service-level escalation, or a separate Support Agent role.
- Professional Connection proposals, consent, connection discovery, or connection lifecycle; these belong to Feature 011.
