# Quickstart: Company Member Management

1. Sign in as the active Owner of a verified company and open `/recruiter/company-settings/team`.
2. Invite an existing account as Recruiter; verify it appears Pending.
3. Sign in as that account and accept the invitation; verify an Active Recruiter membership.
4. As Owner, change the member to HR Manager, suspend, restore, then remove them; verify access changes after each action.
5. Attempt each management endpoint as the member and from another company; verify neutral denial.
6. Run focused contract, service, security, and component tests plus typecheck.
