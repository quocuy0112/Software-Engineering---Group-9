# Job Post Review Usability Protocol

1. Start from a signed-in Recruiter workspace with at least one draft, one pending review, and one rejected posting.
2. Ask the participant to save a draft and submit it for review without assistance.
3. Ask the participant to open the pending review state, read the rejection feedback, revise the posting, and resubmit it.
4. Ask the participant to switch to an Administrator console, locate a pending review, and complete a claim plus decision.
5. Record whether the participant completed each task on the first attempt, the total time, and any blocking confusion.

Capture the raw evidence in the companion results file without rewriting participant observations.
